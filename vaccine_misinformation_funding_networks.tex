\section*{Scope and evidentiary conventions}
This table maps organizations and funding relationships documented in publicly available
tax records, audited financial statements, investigative reporting, and European or
U.S. institutional investigations.  It does \emph{not} assume that every statement
made by an organization listed here is false, nor that the existence of a funding
relationship proves that a donor directed the recipient's scientific or political
positions.

The terms ``misinformation'', ``vaccine-skeptical'', and ``anti-vaccine'' are used
descriptively and are attributed to the cited investigations where appropriate.
Claim-level scientific assessment should be performed separately for particular
claims.  Funding amounts are time-specific and should not be extrapolated to the
present without checking newer filings.

\section*{Interpretive notes}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item The clearest documented financial relationships in this table are the Selz
Foundation's historical support of ICAN and projects associated with Andrew Wakefield,
Joseph Mercola's historical and FY2024 support of NVIC, and the large contribution
base reported by Children's Health Defense.
\item The European evidence is strongest for organizational expansion and lobbying:
EU DisinfoLab documented CHD's Belgian structure, events, and engagement with the
European Parliament.  This should not be converted into a claim that all European
vaccine misinformation is centrally controlled from the United States.
\item The 2025 Associated Press investigation documents a newer political layer in
which MAHA Action, Stand for Health Freedom, NVIC, and the Weston A. Price Foundation
supported or opposed state legislation.  This is evidence of political mobilization,
not by itself evidence about the truth or falsity of every medical claim made by
those organizations.
\item ECDC's EU/EEA review emphasizes that vaccine misinformation has heterogeneous
sources and mechanisms.  Consequently, a responsible research project should avoid
assigning a single ``sponsor'' to the entire European misinformation ecosystem.
\end{enumerate}
